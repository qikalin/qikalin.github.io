Qika Lin

Research Fellow
National University of Singapore (NUS)

Hi! I am Qika Lin, a Research Fellow at the National University of Singapore, working with Prof. Mengling Feng on smartphone triage for pre-hospital emergency care. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology from Xi’an Jiaotong University in 2023, advised by Prof. Jun Liu. From 2021 to 2022, I was a visiting Ph.D. student at Nanyang Technological University, working with Prof. Erik Cambria. The ultimate goal of my research is to develop AI systems that can perceive, reason, and plan in real-world scenarios like humans. Please visit my Google Scholar profile for a complete, up-to-date list of my research publications.

Research Interests

Large Language Models, LLM-Based Agents, Multimodal Reasoning, Neuro-Symbolic Reasoning, Robust AI, and AI for Science.
